# Information Theory

---
[[20240320-083731-probability-thoery#probability-thoery|Probability Theory]] is the basis for another framework called ==Information Theory== which quantifies the information present in a dataset. ==Information Theory== turns out to be of central importance to the field of [[20240325-101235-machine-learning|Machine Learning]].
