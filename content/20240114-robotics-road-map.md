# Ventures

---

## Offer to work for free temporarily at night?

Network with employees from local robotics companies to get more detail about which problems they are working on & tailor my free time attempting to solve those problems. Speak with robotics labs at UT about what they have going on & if they're looking for labor. Maybe, after having read the papers from Zuke's course, it might make sense to try that direction.

## RoboRank: Robotics Puzzle Challenges

This seems like it could be a legit business concept. The robotics literature is dense & the startup cost is high. If we could make it super easy to get started & grow, I could see people flocking to a platform in the mold of HackerRank or LeetCode. I might be able to crowdsource the solutions to difficult problems using the platform.

## DARPA SBIR Grants

DoD lists topics @[SBIR Grant Topics](https://www.dodsbirsttr.mil/topics-app) & provides money to the winning proposals.

## Influencer/Author

These are difficult skills to learn & there might be potential here for sharing them with other people through multiple distribution channels. After I get up to speed with different robotics problems, I could invite technologists & founders on to have intelligent conversations concern them. This would be useful in general from the standpoint of gaining experience towards expertice.

## AI Agents for Games

This is a good abstraction behind which one could really gain mastery of AI algorithms.
