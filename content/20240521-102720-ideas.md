## Explanations

What if an AI could take in any medium & begin to explain it to you from the fundamentals of whatever field is appropriate for the subject? What if that same agent could work with you through multimodal media to bring you closer & closer to mastery in an iterative fashion.

## Master Christian Principals

What if the [[#Explanations]] idea could be applied to help obtain biblical mastery. Conceptual mastery verses mastery of biblical information.

- [ ] Translate all christian literature into modern English & use it as data for pre-training.

- What research is there in this area?
- Hand annotate a dataset from C.S. Lewis & Tim Keller that would allow us to do this?

## Kitchen Orchestrator

Given a kitchen & video/audio feeds, coordinate a group of people through the process of cooking a complicated meal. Find the optimal path to completion. Help ensure that everything is monitored & avoid burning things. Learn everyone's name & create a fun collaborative atmosphere. Teach about food, culture, & history while moving the group through meal preparation. Lead wine & food tastings. Pick experiences that tie into the evening's entertainment. Quiz the group on the names of ingredients, pronunciation, history, & culture. Play music that fits the theme in the space between instruction.

## Laboratory Assistant

Given a physical task that the user needs to perform & audio/visual feeds, walk the user through the process of completing the task. E.g. change the oil in X car || follow some chemical process to synthesize Y.

## Content Creation Augmentation

Help talented content creators bootstrap new content & ideas in order to greatly increase their productivity. Create scripts for ideas that could be viral & allow creators to choose from among them. Help things that are good & beneficial go viral instead of just cheap sexual or violent content.

## Artificial Intelligence Company

Create a company whose overarching objective is to develop AI systems that greatly augment humans to be able to perform more difficult tasks instead of trying to replace humans, turning them into eternal hedonists.
